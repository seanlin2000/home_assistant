## Aftermath
The Warriors made it to a fifth consecutive Finals in 2019, which they would lose to the Toronto Raptors in six games. Three years later, they made it to their sixth Finals in eight seasons and defeated the Boston Celtics in six games.
Despite their dust up that led to Draymond Green's pivotal suspension in Game 5, James and Green would become good friends off the court in the years afterwards. In 2019, Green signed with Klutch Sports, an agency led by LeBron James’ best friend and agent, Rich Paul. Green would later admit he cost the Warriors the Finals with his suspension.
This was the first championship won by an Ohio-based team since 1990, when the Cincinnati Reds won their fifth and most recent World Series title. The win by the Cavaliers remains, as of 2026, the only professional sports championship won in the city of Cleveland in the 21st century. LeBron James, Richard Jefferson, and Kevin Love cheered on the Cleveland Indians in Game 7 of the 2016 World Series against the Chicago Cubs, but Cleveland lost the game in extra-innings.
