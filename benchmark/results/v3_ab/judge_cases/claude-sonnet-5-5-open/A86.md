Reference sketch (what a correct answer must contain):
The Hue Bridge is the local hub: it talks to the bulbs over Zigbee and keeps scenes and automations. Share control by inviting the other people in the Hue app or letting them use it on the home network, add physical Hue switches for anyone without a phone, and connect a voice assistant (Apple Home through the bridge, Alexa, Google Home, or a local controller such as Home Assistant). If the internet goes down: the bridge, its automations, the Hue app on the local network and physical switches keep working; Apple Home with a home hub keeps working locally; cloud voice assistants such as Alexa and Google stop taking commands, and control from outside the home stops. A local voice setup avoids that dependency.

# Exchange 1

USER: I have Philips Hue lights connected to a Hue Bridge. I want multiple people in my apartment to control the lights, including through voice commands. What is the best architecture for this, and what happens if the internet goes down?

ASSISTANT: The best setup is to keep the Hue Bridge on your home network and control it through a hub that everyone shares. For people, install the Hue app on each person's phone and invite them to your Hue home, so everyone can control the lights from their own device. For voice, connect the bridge to Alexa, Google Home or Apple Home, since Hue works with all three, and add everyone to that household. For the most reliable local setup, Home Assistant can talk to the bridge directly over your local network. If the internet goes down, the bridge still controls the lights locally, so the Hue app on your Wi-Fi and physical dimmer switches or smart buttons keep working, and timers and schedules stored on the bridge keep running. Apple Home and Home Assistant also keep working locally. What stops is anything that depends on the cloud, which usually means Alexa and Google voice commands and remote control while you are away. If offline voice matters, use Apple Home with a HomePod or Apple TV hub, or a local voice setup in Home Assistant.
