# Exchange 1

USER: How did the Cold War end? Explain the three most important events that led to the dissolution of the Soviet Union, and distinguish between the causes and the immediate triggers.

ASSISTANT: The Cold War ended because the Soviet system could no longer sustain itself, and then a short chain of events finished it off in 1991. Start with the causes, which were slow. The Soviet economy stagnated under central planning and an enormous military burden, and the war in Afghanistan drained money and morale. Then Gorbachev's reforms, glasnost and perestroika, loosened control without fixing the economy, which let long-suppressed grievances out. Now the three events that mattered most. First, in 1989 the Eastern European communist governments fell one after another, and Moscow chose not to send tanks, so the Berlin Wall opened and Germany reunified in 1990. Second, the Soviet republics, starting with the Baltics and then Russia under Boris Yeltsin, asserted independence, which hollowed out the union from within. Third, the failed hardliner coup in August 1991 was the immediate trigger. It discredited the Communist Party, boosted Yeltsin, and within months the republics left, and the Soviet Union was formally dissolved in December 1991. So the causes were economic decay and political opening, while the coup was the spark that made collapse unstoppable.
