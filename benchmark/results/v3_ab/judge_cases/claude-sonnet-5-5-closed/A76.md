# Exchange 1

USER: How did the Cold War end? Explain the three most important events that led to the dissolution of the Soviet Union, and distinguish between the causes and the immediate triggers.

ASSISTANT: The Cold War ended because the Soviet Union lost the ability and the will to hold its system and its empire together, and it ended peacefully between 1989 and 1991. Three events mattered most. First, Mikhail Gorbachev's reforms, glasnost and perestroika, from 1985 on, which opened up criticism and loosened central control without fixing the economy. Second, the revolutions of 1989 in Eastern Europe, capped by the fall of the Berlin Wall, when Gorbachev refused to send tanks to prop up allied governments. Third, the failed hardliner coup of August 1991, which discredited the Communist Party and handed power to Boris Yeltsin and the republics. Now the distinction. The deep causes were a stagnant, overcentralized economy, the cost of the arms race and the war in Afghanistan, falling oil revenue in the 1980s, and rising nationalism in the republics. The immediate triggers were the coup attempt, the Baltic and Ukrainian moves toward independence, and the December 1991 agreement among Russia, Ukraine and Belarus to dissolve the union, after which Gorbachev resigned on December twenty-fifth.
