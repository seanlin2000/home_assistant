What is the Fed Funds Rate?
The Federal Funds Rate is the interest rate at which banks lend money to each other overnight — set by the Federal Reserve as its primary monetary policy tool. Most other US interest rates key off it: mortgages, car loans, credit cards, Treasury yields and corporate bonds. So it sets the cost of borrowing for households and businesses across the economy.
