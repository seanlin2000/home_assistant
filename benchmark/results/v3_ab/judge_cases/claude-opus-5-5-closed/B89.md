### How to read private-market numbers
The round amount is capital invested at a particular date. The post-money valuation reflects transaction pricing at that point in time. Neither equals annual revenue, assets under management or investor cash distributions. Terms can differ by share class. Deal announcements, preliminary negotiations and closed acquisitions are distinct events; figures in this article are not aggregated into an artificial market-total.
