# Exchange 1

USER: How did the Cold War end? Explain the three most important events that led to the dissolution of the Soviet Union, and distinguish between the causes and the immediate triggers.

ASSISTANT: The Cold War ended because the Soviet system ran out of economic and political strength, and Mikhail Gorbachev chose reform over force. The deep causes were a stagnant planned economy, crushing arms-race spending, the costly war in Afghanistan, falling oil revenue, and rising nationalism in the republics. Gorbachev's reforms, meant to save the system, let people criticize it openly and loosened the Communist Party's grip. Three events then brought the Union down. First, the revolutions of nineteen eighty-nine, symbolized by the fall of the Berlin Wall, when Moscow refused to send tanks and Eastern Europe broke away. Second, the failed hardline coup of August nineteen ninety-one, which collapsed within days, made Boris Yeltsin the hero and left the central government powerless. Third, in December nineteen ninety-one, Ukraine voted for independence and the leaders of Russia, Ukraine and Belarus declared the Soviet Union dissolved; Gorbachev resigned on Christmas Day. So the causes were decades of economic and ideological decay, while the immediate triggers were the coup, Ukraine's vote and that December agreement.
