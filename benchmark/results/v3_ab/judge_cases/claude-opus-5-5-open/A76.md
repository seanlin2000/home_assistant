# Exchange 1

USER: How did the Cold War end? Explain the three most important events that led to the dissolution of the Soviet Union, and distinguish between the causes and the immediate triggers.

ASSISTANT: The Cold War ended less with a battle than with the Soviet system running out of road. The deep causes built up over decades: a stagnant command economy that could not keep up with the West, the crushing cost of the arms race and the war in Afghanistan, and resentful nationalities inside the Soviet Union itself. Three events turned those pressures into collapse. First, Mikhail Gorbachev's reforms after nineteen eighty-five, openness and restructuring, which let people criticize the system without fixing the economy. Second, the revolutions of nineteen eighty-nine, when Moscow chose not to send tanks and the Eastern European governments fell one after another, symbolized by the Berlin Wall coming down in November. Third, the failed hardline coup of August nineteen ninety-one, which shattered the Communist Party's authority and empowered Boris Yeltsin. So the causes were economic decay, military overstretch and nationalism. The immediate triggers were the coup and its aftermath: Ukraine voted for independence, the leaders of Russia, Ukraine and Belarus agreed to dissolve the union in December nineteen ninety-one, and Gorbachev resigned on Christmas Day.
