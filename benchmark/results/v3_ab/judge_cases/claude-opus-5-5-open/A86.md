Reference sketch (what a correct answer must contain):
The Hue Bridge is the local hub: it talks to the bulbs over Zigbee and keeps scenes and automations. Share control by inviting the other people in the Hue app or letting them use it on the home network, add physical Hue switches for anyone without a phone, and connect a voice assistant (Apple Home through the bridge, Alexa, Google Home, or a local controller such as Home Assistant). If the internet goes down: the bridge, its automations, the Hue app on the local network and physical switches keep working; Apple Home with a home hub keeps working locally; cloud voice assistants such as Alexa and Google stop taking commands, and control from outside the home stops. A local voice setup avoids that dependency.

# Exchange 1

USER: I have Philips Hue lights connected to a Hue Bridge. I want multiple people in my apartment to control the lights, including through voice commands. What is the best architecture for this, and what happens if the internet goes down?

ASSISTANT: Keep the Hue Bridge as the heart of it. It runs the lights over its own local Zigbee network, and everything else talks to it. For several people, the simplest route is to add the bridge to one shared smart-home platform, Apple Home, Google Home or Alexa, through Matter or their Hue integrations, and invite each person to that home, so everyone gets app and voice control under their own account. If you want more control and privacy, run Home Assistant on a small always-on computer, connect it to the bridge locally, and use it as the hub for dashboards, automations and a local voice assistant. Also put Hue dimmer switches by the doors, so guests never need an app. If the internet goes down, the bridge, the physical switches and control over your home Wi-Fi keep working, including Home Assistant with local voice, and Apple Home with a home hub like an Apple TV or HomePod. What fails is control from outside the apartment and cloud voice assistants like Alexa and Google, which need the internet to understand you.
