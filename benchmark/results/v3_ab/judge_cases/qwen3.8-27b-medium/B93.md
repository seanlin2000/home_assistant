The LIC location has about fifteen dishes you won't get at the original East Village spot, inspired by Bangkok's Chinatown, and they still serve what many people call the city's best khao soi. Expect to land around thirty to fifty dollars a person, and come hungry — the whole dungeness crab in orange curry is meant to be torn apart.

Second, Casa Enrique, a Mexican institution on Vernon Boulevard. Crunchy fish tacos, spicy ceviche, and a rich mole de Pachuca, plus seasonal watermelon margaritas at the big bar where you can watch soccer.
