## How to Buy Cheap Tickets to a Broadway Show
Broadway tickets can be expensive, averaging about $131 per person during the 2025–2026 season, but there are plenty of ways to catch a show without giving your wallet stage fright.
One of the most classic ways to score cheaper seats is by lining up at the bright red TKTS Booth on West 47th Street between Broadway and Seventh Avenue, where you can snag discounted tickets for same-day performances and next-day matinees. You can also check the TKTS app for real-time availability before heading there. Popular shows can disappear quickly, though new seats sometimes pop up throughout the day as theaters release more inventory. You can purchase up to six tickets at once, and while there is an $8 service charge per ticket, it is still lower than the fees charged by many online sellers. TDF also operates a second TKTS Booth inside Lincoln Center’s David Rubenstein Atrium.
