## Who is Chase Premier Savings best for?
The Chase Premier Savings account only makes sense for existing Chase customers who want to keep all their accounts in one place, because one of the ways you can waive the fee is by linking another Chase account to it. And with a $25 monthly maintenance fee, you’ll want to get that waived. You can also waive the fee with a balance of at least $15,000 at the beginning of each day.
## What we like about Chase Premier Savings
There isn’t much good to say, but it’s worth noting that the Premier Savings account doesn’t have any balance requirements to earn either the 0.01% to 0.02% APY.
But there are requirements to earn the slightly higher 0.02% APY, called the “relationship” rate. Requirements include:
If you’re an existing Chase customer and the checking account you’re linking is your everyday checking account, then meeting the transaction requirements is easy. By linking an account to get the relationship rate, you’d also waive the Premier Savings monthly fee, so it’s two birds with one stone.
