## How We Calculate
- Amount in USD = Amount in EUR × Exchange Rate
- Inverse Rate = 1 ÷ Exchange Rate (value of 1 USD in EUR)
## Worked Example
### €100 at a rate of 1.08 USD per EUR
Amount in USD = €100 × 1.08 = `$108.00`.
The inverse rate is 1 ÷ 1.08 ≈ `€0.9259`, meaning 1 USD is worth about 0.93 euros at this rate.

## Do banks add a markup to the market rate?
Yes, typically. Banks and card networks commonly add a spread or fee on top of the mid-market rate you see quoted online, so the rate you actually get when exchanging euros for dollars is often slightly less favorable than the headline market rate.
