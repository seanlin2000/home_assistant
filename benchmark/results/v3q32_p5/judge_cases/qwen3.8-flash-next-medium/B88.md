## Muse Spark Open and Llama 4 (Meta)
Muse Spark Open is Meta’s first large open-weight release since Llama 4. It shares the `meta-models` Hugging Face organization with Muse Glimmer 30B, the dense model Meta published in August, but it ships under a license of its own. That organization carries Hugging Face’s verified badge under the name Meta Inc. The card credits Meta Superintelligence Labs and dates the release 15 October 2026. It is an 816B mixture-of-experts with 42B active, 256 experts routed 8 at a time across 62 layers, a 1M token context, and a 1.8B perception encoder, with text, image, video and audio input. Access is gated: you fill in a form with your full legal name, date of birth and organization, and Meta approves it by hand.
## Mistral Models
A new open-weight family is coming: Mistral confirmed in early July 2026 that a larger “fat but sparse” MoE family entered early access with research and government partners, with no public parameter count or ship date. Benchmarks have since surfaced without weights: Meta’s Muse card scores a “Mistral Large 4” against its own model, and Artificial Analysis lists a Mistral Large 4 Preview on 38, but no Large 4 weights sit in Mistral’s Hugging Face organization as of October 2026. The original Mistral 7B and Mixtral 8x7B remain in wide deployment and are still serviceable Apache 2.0 fallbacks on old hardware, though every current alternative outperforms them.
