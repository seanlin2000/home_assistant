ASSISTANT: For local large language models, the bottleneck isn't how fast the GPU calculates, but whether it has enough video memory to hold the model and its active context at all. If your VRAM is too small, the system must offload layers to your slow system RAM, which can degrade performance by an order of magnitude or more. So while a faster GPU helps with speed once everything fits, having adequate VRAM capacity is the absolute prerequisite for running modern models smoothly on your machine.
