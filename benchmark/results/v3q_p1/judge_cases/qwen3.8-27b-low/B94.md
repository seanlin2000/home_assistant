## The break-even math, in one line
The card costs CHF 150 and saves you half of every fare, so it pays for itself once you would have spent CHF 300 on full-fare tickets within the month. That is the entire calculation. Everything else is just working out whether your trip reaches CHF 300.
The trap runs the other way too. A trip built around one long transfer and a lot of walking may never reach CHF 300, and then the CHF 150 is simply money spent.
## When the Half Fare Card is the wrong choice
- You plan to ride trains on most days. Daily train travel across a week almost always favours the Swiss Travel Pass, which also covers museums and includes some mountain routes outright rather than discounting them.

Most mountain railways and cable cars give the full 50% discount with the Half Fare Card — including many routes where the Swiss Travel Pass only gives 25–50%. For Jungfraujoch specifically, the Half Fare Card discount is often the stronger of the two.
### Is the Half Fare Card a ticket?
