The flow: the mic array hears your wake word, openWakeWord flags it, whisper transcribes the question, Ollama runs your Qwen model as the conversation agent, and that agent can call tools — Home Assistant for device control, and a search or knowledge tool for general questions. The spoken answer comes back through Piper, which is fast but a bit robotic, or Kokoro, which sounds much more natural and still runs fast on that GPU. You could actually run everything on one box, but separating the always-on mic from the GPU box means the GPU can sleep and you get a cleaner audio path.
