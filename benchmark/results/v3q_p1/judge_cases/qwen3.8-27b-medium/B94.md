Short answer: in Switzerland, three tools bring down the price of train travel, and they don't target the same travellers. ## What is the Half Fare Card (Halbtax)?
The Half Fare Card is a personal travelcard that gives you roughly 50% off the price of most public transport in Switzerland for a full year. It’s not a ticket itself — you still buy a ticket for each journey — but every ticket you buy is charged at half the normal price.
Think of it as a membership card: pay once a year, then pay half price on nearly everything you ride.
