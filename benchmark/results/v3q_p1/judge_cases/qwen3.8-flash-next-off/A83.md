After 50 percent cumulative dilution, that stake is cut in half to roughly 0.0167 percent. At a one billion dollar exit, that's worth about $166,667 before taxes.

One thing to keep in mind is what "50 percent cumulative dilution" means here — I read it as your ownership being halved across all the later rounds combined. If instead it meant each round diluted you by 50 percent, or that the total share count doubled at some specific stage, the answer would shift. Also worth noting this assumes a straight pro rata payout with no liquidation preferences ahead of you; in a real cap table, preferred holders could take a chunk off the top before common shares like yours see anything.
