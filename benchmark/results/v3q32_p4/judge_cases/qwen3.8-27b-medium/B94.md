## The Half Fare Card alternative
The Half Fare Card (Halbtax) costs CHF 150 for 1 month and halves the price of almost all Swiss rail tickets. It does not cover city transport, lake boats, or museums. It does not cover mountain railways.
When should you choose the Half Fare Card over the Swiss Travel Pass?
- If you are making only 2–3 long-distance journeys and not using city transport
- If you do not plan mountain excursions
- If you are staying mostly in one place and moving only occasionally
When the Swiss Travel Pass is clearly better:
- Any trip with 3+ travel days and regular movement
- Any trip including mountain excursions

## Frequently asked questions about the Swiss Travel Pass
### Swiss Travel Pass vs Half Fare Card: which is better?
The Swiss Travel Pass is better for trips of 4+ days with regular movement between cities, mountain excursions, and scenic trains. The Half Fare Card (CHF 150 for one month) is better for shorter trips with only 2-3 long-distance journeys and no mountain excursions. The pass includes city transport and museums; the Half Fare Card does not.
### What’s the minimum number of days that makes the pass worth it?
As a rough rule, 4 or more active travel days with movement between at least 3 destinations and 1-2 mountain excursions is where the pass typically starts beating individually bought tickets. Shorter or slower trips often do better with the Half Fare Card instead.

## Consecutive pass or Swiss Travel Pass Flex?
A consecutive pass runs on successive days. Flex offers 3, 4, 6, 8 or 15 selected travel days within one month. Choose Flex only after marking the days when you need its transport and attraction benefits. Follow the activation instructions supplied with that product before traveling on a selected day.
## What is included, and what is extra?
Keep three costs separate: transport fare, compulsory or optional seat reservation, and any mountain or special-service supplement. The Glacier Express and Bernina Express panoramic services require reservations even when the pass covers the transport fare. Verify the class and travel day on both documents.
## Eligibility and children
The Swiss Travel Pass is intended for people permanently resident outside Switzerland and Liechtenstein. Children aged 6 to under 16 can use the free Swiss Family Card when traveling with an eligible parent holding a qualifying Swiss Travel System product. Add children when purchasing and verify the issued documents; a child’s free transport entitlement does not automatically include a reserved seat.
