USER: How did the Cold War end? Explain the three most important events that led to the dissolution of the Soviet Union, and distinguish between the causes and the immediate triggers.

ASSISTANT: The Cold War ended not in a single moment but through a slow unraveling that accelerated in 1989 and 1990.

The deep causes were internal: Gorbachev's reforms, glass and perestroika, which unintentionally loosened Soviet control while the economy stagnated and the war in Afghanistan drained confidence. Chernobyl in 1986 had already shaken faith in the state.

The three most important events were the fall of the Berlin Wall in November 1989, the chain of revolutions across Eastern Europe that year, and the failed hardline coup against Gorbachev in August 1991.

The distinction matters. The reforms, economics, and ideology were the causes. The Berlin Wall and the coup were the immediate triggers: the wall showed the system could be abandoned, and the coup destroyed Gorbachev's last authority, pushing the republics to leave by December 1991.
