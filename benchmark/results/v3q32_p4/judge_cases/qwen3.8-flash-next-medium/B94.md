## Consecutive days or Flex?
The standard pass runs on consecutive days: an 8-day pass covers 8 days in a row. The Swiss Travel Pass Flex (from CHF 289) gives you the same number of travel days to use on any dates within one month — you activate each day before your first ride.
## How many days do you need?
- One-week trip: the 8-day pass is the sweet spot — it costs only CHF 40 more than 6 days and covers arrival and departure days, including your airport trains.
- Two weeks: the 15-day pass at CHF 499 works out under CHF 34 per day — less than a single Zurich–Lucerne return.

## First or second class?
Second class is comfortable, clean and how most Swiss travel — it is the right default. First class buys you quieter cars, wider seats and better odds of a window seat on busy panoramic routes in July–August. If the budget question is “1st class or two extra mountain excursions?”, take the mountains.
