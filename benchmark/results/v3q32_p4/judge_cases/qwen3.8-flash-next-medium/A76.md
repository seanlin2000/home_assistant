# Exchange 1

USER: How did the Cold War end? Explain the three most important events that led to the dissolution of the Soviet Union, and distinguish between the causes and the immediate triggers.

ASSISTANT: The Cold War ended not with a battle but with the Soviet system simply ceasing to hold itself together. The underlying causes were long-run: an economy hollowed out by stagnation and the arms race, and Mikhail Gorbachev's perestroika and glasnost, which loosened central control without reviving legitimacy, emboldening nationalist movements in Ukraine, the Baltics and elsewhere. The immediate triggers were three events. First, the Berlin Wall fell in November 1989, ending Soviet domination of Eastern Europe. Second, the failed hardliner coup against Gorbachev in August 1991 destroyed the Union's remaining authority and elevated Boris Yeltsin. Third, on 8 December 1991 Russia, Ukraine and Belarus signed the Belavezha Accords declaring the USSR dissolved; Gorbachev resigned on 25 December.
