ASSISTANT: The 24 GB card is the better choice for local LLMs, and the difference is more practical than you might expect.

At roughly 4-bit quantization, a 16 GB card tops out around the 27B dense models, and even that is a tight fit where the Q4 build just barely doesn't leave room for context. In practice, 16 GB is comfortable up to about 14 to 20B parameters at decent quantization, which gets you good models but leaves you at the lower end of what's useful.

With 24 GB you land in what most guides now call the sweet spot. You can run 27 to 32B dense models at Q4 with real headroom left over for context window, and you can also fit the larger MoE models, like the 30B-class Qwen variants, at Q4 without squeezing. That means both better base models and the ability to give them longer context, which matters a lot for agentic work, coding, and any task where the model needs to hold a lot of information in play at once.

The rule in this space is simple: if the model plus its KV cache doesn't fit, it doesn't run. So the extra 8 GB isn't just headroom, it's the difference between being able to run the 32B tier at all versus being stuck at 14 to 20B.

If price is close between the two, go 24. If the 24 GB is significantly more expensive and you know you'll be happy with 14 to 20B class models, 16 is still perfectly usable, but you'll hit a ceiling sooner as the open model landscape keeps pushing toward 30B and above.
