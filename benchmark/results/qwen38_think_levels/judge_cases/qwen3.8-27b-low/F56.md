## Bainbridge Island Ferry FAQs
### How long is the ferry ride from Bainbridge to Seattle?
The ferry ride from Bainbridge Island to Seattle is approximately 35 minutes long.
### How long is the ferry ride from Seattle to Bainbridge?
The ferry ride from Seattle to Bainbridge Island is approximately 35 minutes long.
### How early should I arrive for Bainbridge ferry?
You should arrive no later than 20 minutes prior to the ferry's departure time. During the most popular departure times, you should arrive one hour prior to departure.
### What time does the Bainbridge ferry run?
On weekdays the first ferry leaves Bainbridge Island at 4:45 AM and Seattle at 5:30 AM. On weekends and holidays the first ferry leaves Bainbridge Island at 5:20 AM and Seattle at 6:10 AM.
### How late does Bainbridge ferry run?
The last weekday ferry leaves Bainbridge Island at 12:55 AM and Seattle at 1:35 AM, after midnight. On weekends and holidays the last ferry leaves Bainbridge Island at 1:25 AM and Seattle at 2:10 AM.
### Do you pay both ways for Bainbridge ferry?
No. Walk-on passengers pay only from Seattle to Bainbridge Island ($11.35 for an adult). The ferry ride from Bainbridge Island to Seattle is free if you do not have a vehicle. Vehicles and their drivers pay in both directions.
### Is the galley open on Washington State Ferries?
Yes. The boats on the Seattle/Bainbridge ferry this season, the MV Puyallup and MV Wenatchee, have a galley.
