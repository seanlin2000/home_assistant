ASSISTANT: Whole coffee beans taste best within about two to four weeks of their roast date. After that, they lose aroma and can start to taste flat. Ground coffee goes even faster, typically a week or two at most.

The general advice is to store them in an airtight, opaque container at room temperature, away from heat and light. If you're not going to use the beans within a month, freezing them in sealed portions is an option, but the fridge is usually a bad idea because of moisture and odors. The simplest trick is to buy smaller bags more often and grind just before brewing.
