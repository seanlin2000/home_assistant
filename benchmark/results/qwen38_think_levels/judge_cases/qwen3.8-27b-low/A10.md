USER: Explain why VRAM capacity can matter more than raw GPU speed when choosing hardware for a local LLM. Answer in a way that would sound natural if spoken aloud in under 45 seconds.

ASSISTANT: Here's the thing. A local LLM has to keep its entire weights in VRAM to run at full speed. If the model's too big for your card, weights spill into regular RAM, which is maybe ten to twenty times slower because of the PCIe bottleneck. So a fast GPU running a model that doesn't fit will actually lose to a slower GPU that holds the whole thing. Speed only becomes the deciding factor once you've confirmed the model fits with room to spare for your context window. Rule of thumb: VRAM first, speed second.
