# European Central Bank (ECB) Exchange Rates
## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
What are ECB exchange rates?
ECB exchange rates are the reference exchange rates for the Euro published by the European Central Bank (ECB) on a daily basis. These rates are widely used for various financial and commercial purposes.
How often are the ECB exchange rates updated?
The ECB exchange rates are updated daily, typically around 16:00 CET on business days when the ECB is open.
Are ECB exchange rates the same as market exchange rates?
No, ECB exchange rates are reference rates and may differ from market exchange rates, which are determined by supply and demand in the foreign exchange market. Market rates can fluctuate throughout the day, while ECB rates are fixed once daily.
Where can I find historical ECB exchange rates?
Historical ECB rates can be found on this site by clicking the 'graph' icon next to each currency in the rates table, or on the 'History' page accessible from the main menu.
How reliable are ECB exchange rates?
ECB exchange rates are considered reliable and authoritative as they are published by the European Central Bank, a reputable institution. However, for real-time currency conversion, market rates should be consulted.
Can I get ECB exchange rates in other languages?
Yes, ECB exchange rates are available in multiple languages. You can switch languages using the language selector in the app.
How can I get ECB exchange rates for a specific date?
You can select a specific date using the date selector on the homepage to view the ECB exchange rates published on that date.
What is happening if there is no ECB exchange rate for a selected date?
If there is no ECB exchange rate for the selected date (for example, weekends or public holidays), the most recent previous date with available rates will be used instead.
