## Mount Pilatus
The cogwheel railway is the steepest in the world, with a gradient of up to 48 percent, and runs from 11 May to 29 November 2026. It does not run in winter. The gondolas and cableway are closed for maintenance from 19 October to 27 November 2026, and the boat to Alpnachstad stops on 18 October. Current fares and dates are on the Pilatus price page .
## Stanserhorn CabriO
The Stanserhorn trip starts with a 13 minute Zentralbahn ride from Lucerne to Stans, then the historic funicular and the open-top double-decker CabriO cable car to the summit at 1,898 metres. The return costs CHF 82, or CHF 41 with a Half Fare card, and it is free with the Swiss Travel Pass and Lucerne Travel Pass. The 2026 season runs from 11 April to 22 November. See the Stanserhorn price page .
## Which pass
The Swiss Travel Pass covers Rigi and the Stanserhorn but gives only half price on Pilatus and Titlis. The regional Lucerne Travel Pass, which replaced the Tell-Pass on 1 April 2026, covers all four. It is sold for 3, 4, 5 or 10 consecutive days at about CHF 240, 265, 290 and 365, and the Swiss Travel Pass gives no discount on it. Seat reservations on Pilatus are separate.
