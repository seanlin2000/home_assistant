The short version from several hardware-checker sites: a standard Q4 quantization of Qwen3.8 27B needs roughly seventeen to eighteen gigabytes of VRAM once you add the KV cache and runtime overhead, which is more than the sixteen gigabytes on a 4060 Ti. So at that quant level, it doesn't fit cleanly. The sites do note it can be squeezed onto a 16 GB card in two ways: either drop to a lower quant like Q3 or IQ3_XS (around thirteen to fourteen gigabytes), which then fits with room for some context, or offload part of the model to system RAM, which one source estimated at around eleven tokens per second.

Now, is that enough to predict whether it will give you a good voice-assistant experience? Honestly, no, and here's why.

First, "can it run" and "will it feel good as a voice assistant" are different questions. A voice assistant has a latency budget measured in hundreds of milliseconds before a human notices the pause. Even if the model runs at a comfortable thirty tokens per second when it fits on GPU, that's still several seconds to generate a spoken reply, before you even count speech-to-text and text-to-speech. The search results give you memory fit and rough throughput numbers, but nothing about end-to-end latency for a voice pipeline, which is the thing that actually determines whether it feels responsive or sluggish.

Second, the sources are split on exactly what fits, and they're estimates, not measurements. One site says Q4 needs eighteen point three gigabytes at eight thousand context; another says seventeen point three at four thousand context; another claims a 3-bit quant runs fine. Those numbers vary by up to fifteen percent depending on context length and runtime, which means the "does it fit" answer is genuinely uncertain rather than settled.

Third, none of these results address what a voice assistant actually needs: short, fast responses with low latency, not long-context generation. A 27B model might be overkill for that use case entirely, and a smaller model that fits comfortably at Q4 would likely feel better in a voice loop than a 27B model squeezed into 3-bit quant or spilling into system RAM.

So the search tells you whether the card has enough memory, which is a necessary but not sufficient answer.
