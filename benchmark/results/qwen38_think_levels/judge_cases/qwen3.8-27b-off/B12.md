The best value in raw VRAM per dollar is still a used RTX 3090 with its twenty-four gigabytes of GDDR6X. It comfortably runs thirty-billion-parameter models at four-bit quantization, and it's well supported by Ollama, LM Studio and llama.cpp. The catch is price: completed eBay sales over the last two weeks average around sixteen hundred dollars, and asking prices run fifteen to nineteen hundred. That alone blows your whole budget before you have a motherboard, CPU, RAM or power supply, so I can't honestly tell you to build around it today.

So what actually fits in a thousand? You're looking at sixteen gigabytes of VRAM as the realistic ceiling.
