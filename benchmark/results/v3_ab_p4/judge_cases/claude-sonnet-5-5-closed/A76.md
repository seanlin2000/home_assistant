USER: How did the Cold War end? Explain the three most important events that led to the dissolution of the Soviet Union, and distinguish between the causes and the immediate triggers.

ASSISTANT: The Cold War ended because the Soviet system ran out of the money, legitimacy and will to hold its empire together, and it ended peacefully. The three events I would stress are these. First, Mikhail Gorbachev's reforms, glasnost and perestroika, from 1985 on, which opened up criticism and weakened the party's grip without fixing the economy. Second, the 1989 revolutions in Eastern Europe, capped by the fall of the Berlin Wall, when Gorbachev refused to send tanks and the satellite regimes collapsed. Third, the failed hardliner coup of August 1991, which discredited the Communist Party and handed power to Boris Yeltsin and the republics. The causes were deep and slow: a stagnant planned economy, the cost of the arms race and the war in Afghanistan, falling oil prices, and rising nationalism in the republics. The immediate triggers were the things that set off the collapse: the coup attempt, the republics declaring independence, and the December 1991 agreement by Russia, Ukraine and Belarus to dissolve the union. Gorbachev resigned on December 25, 1991.
