# Exchange 1

USER: How did the Cold War end? Explain the three most important events that led to the dissolution of the Soviet Union, and distinguish between the causes and the immediate triggers.

ASSISTANT: The Cold War ended largely peacefully between nineteen eighty-nine and nineteen ninety-one, first with Eastern Europe breaking away and then with the Soviet Union itself dissolving. The deep causes were slow-building: a stagnant planned economy, the cost of the arms race, the war in Afghanistan, collapsing oil revenue, and rising nationalism in the republics. Three events mattered most. First, Mikhail Gorbachev's reforms from nineteen eighty-five, glasnost and perestroika, which loosened censorship and exposed how badly the system was failing. Second, the revolutions of nineteen eighty-nine, when Gorbachev refused to send in tanks and the Berlin Wall fell, proving Moscow would no longer hold its empire by force. Third, the failed hardline coup of August nineteen ninety-one, which collapsed within days, discredited the Communist Party and handed power to Boris Yeltsin and the republics. So the causes were economic and political decay; the immediate triggers were the coup and its aftermath, when Russia, Ukraine and Belarus agreed that December to dissolve the union and Gorbachev resigned on Christmas Day.
