# Exchange 1

USER: My projector is connected to a JBL Bluetooth speaker. Sometimes the audio becomes choppy when I connect my laptop, and playing video games on the projector causes more eye strain than playing on my monitor. What could cause these problems, and how would you systematically troubleshoot them?

ASSISTANT: There are two separate problems here. For the choppy audio, the likely causes are Bluetooth radio interference, since Bluetooth shares the crowded two point four gigahertz band with Wi-Fi, distance or obstacles between the laptop and speaker, a laptop that is also busy with Wi-Fi traffic, a weak or outdated Bluetooth driver, or the speaker being paired with two devices at once. Another common cause is the projector's own Bluetooth transmitter competing with the laptop. To troubleshoot, change one thing at a time. First, move the laptop and speaker closer, then disconnect the projector's Bluetooth link. Next, test the speaker with just the laptop, then with just the projector. Switch the laptop to five gigahertz Wi-Fi or use ethernet, update drivers, and try a wired audio connection to see if the problem disappears. For the eye strain, a projector is a large, bright, low-contrast image in a dim room, which can fatigue eyes, and there can be input lag or lower refresh rate. Try dimming or raising the room light, lowering projector brightness, using eco mode, checking focus and resolution, and switching it into game mode to cut lag.
