# Exchange 1

USER: How did the Cold War end? Explain the three most important events that led to the dissolution of the Soviet Union, and distinguish between the causes and the immediate triggers.

ASSISTANT: The Cold War ended because the Soviet Union stopped being able to compete and then stopped existing, and it helps to separate the deep causes from the triggers. The deep causes were a stagnant command economy that could not keep up with the arms race with the West, the cost of the war in Afghanistan, and a political system that had lost its legitimacy. The three most important events were these. First, Mikhail Gorbachev's reforms of glasnost and perestroika from 1985, which loosened the press and the economy but unleashed criticism and nationalism he could not control. Second, the fall of the Berlin Wall in 1989 and the collapse of the Soviet-backed communist governments across Eastern Europe, after Gorbachev made clear Moscow would not send tanks. That effectively ended the Cold War. Third, the failed hardliner coup in August 1991, which destroyed Gorbachev's authority and the Communist Party's grip. The immediate triggers were that coup, Ukraine's overwhelming vote for independence that December, and Boris Yeltsin and the Russian, Ukrainian and Belarusian leaders agreeing to dissolve the union. The Soviet Union formally ended on December twenty-fifth, 1991.
