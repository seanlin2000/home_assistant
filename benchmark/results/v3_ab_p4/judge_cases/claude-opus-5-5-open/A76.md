# Exchange 1

USER: How did the Cold War end? Explain the three most important events that led to the dissolution of the Soviet Union, and distinguish between the causes and the immediate triggers.

ASSISTANT: The Cold War ended not with a battle but with the Soviet system unraveling between nineteen eighty-five and nineteen ninety-one. Three events mattered most. First, Mikhail Gorbachev's reforms, glasnost and perestroika, which loosened censorship and tried to fix a stagnant economy, but instead exposed its failures and let criticism and nationalism pour out. Second, the revolutions of nineteen eighty-nine, when Moscow chose not to send tanks, so the Eastern European communist governments fell one after another and the Berlin Wall opened. Third, the failed hardline coup of August nineteen ninety-one, which discredited the Communist Party and handed power to Boris Yeltsin and the republics. The deeper causes were long-running: an inefficient planned economy, the crushing cost of the arms race and the war in Afghanistan, falling oil revenue, and independence movements inside the republics. The immediate triggers came at the very end: the coup's collapse, Ukraine's vote for independence on December first, nineteen ninety-one, the agreement a week later in which Russia, Ukraine and Belarus declared the union dissolved, and Gorbachev's resignation on December twenty-fifth.
