## Planning your trip around the pass
The pass rewards trips built around the rail network. The classic first-timer loop — Zurich, Lucerne, Interlaken, Zermatt — is entirely covered, and the Grand Train Tour of Switzerland strings the best panoramic routes into one circuit you can ride on a single 8-day pass.
## When the Swiss Travel Pass is the wrong buy
- A short trip with only a few journeys. Three or four days in Switzerland with two train rides and a lot of walking rarely reaches the value of a pass. A Half Fare Card at CHF 150 plus half-price tickets, or even plain point-to-point tickets, usually costs less.
- Your trip is built around one or two big mountains. This one surprises people. On several mountain railways the Half Fare Card gives 50% off, while the Swiss Travel Pass gives 25–50% depending on the route. For a trip whose main cost is one expensive peak, the cheaper card can be the better card.
