### Lucerne Travel Pass (formerly Tell-Pass)
The Tell-Pass was renamed the Lucerne Travel Pass on 1 April 2026, with new year-round pricing: 3 days CHF 240, 4 days CHF 265, 5 days CHF 290, 10 days CHF 365, and a flat CHF 30 for children aged 6 to 16 (Lucerne Travel Pass).
