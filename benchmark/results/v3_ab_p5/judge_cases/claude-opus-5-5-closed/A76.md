ASSISTANT: The Cold War ended because the Soviet system ran out of money and legitimacy, and a few events between nineteen eighty-nine and nineteen ninety-one finished it. The deep causes were a stagnant command economy, the cost of the arms race and the Afghan war, falling oil revenue, and rising nationalism in the republics. The first key event was Mikhail Gorbachev's reforms after nineteen eighty-five, glasnost and perestroika, which loosened censorship and central control and let independence movements surface. The second was the revolutions of nineteen eighty-nine in Eastern Europe, symbolized by the Berlin Wall falling, when Moscow declined to send tanks and the satellite states slipped away. The third was the failed hardline coup of August nineteen ninety-one, which collapsed in three days, made Boris Yeltsin the hero, and broke the Communist Party's authority. The distinction is this: the economy, the arms race and nationalism were causes, while the coup and Ukraine's independence vote that December were the immediate triggers. Gorbachev resigned on Christmas Day, nineteen ninety-one, and the union dissolved.
