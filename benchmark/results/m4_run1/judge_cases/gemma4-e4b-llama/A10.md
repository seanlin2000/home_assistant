ASSISTANT: VRAM capacity is crucial for local LLMs because the entire language model, or at least large parts of it, needs to fit into the GPU's memory to run efficiently. If the model is too large for the VRAM, you have to offload parts of it to the system RAM or even your CPU, which is much slower and drastically slows down generation. So, even a faster GPU won't help much if it can't hold the model in the first place.
