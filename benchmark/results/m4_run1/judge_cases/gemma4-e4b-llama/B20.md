A voice satellite is a small microphone-and-speaker endpoint that puts Home Assistant's Assist voice control in a room while the intelligence runs elsewhere. HA's docs describe satellites as devices that add Assist with wake words to all your rooms: the satellite hears the wake word, streams your command over Wyoming to the HA server, and plays back the reply once the server's speech-to-text, intent handling, and text-to-speech finish. Because it is just an audio endpoint, the hardware can be a $13 ESP32 puck or a retired Raspberry Pi, and one server feeds every room.
