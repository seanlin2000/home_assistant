ASSISTANT: The RTX 4060 Ti with 16 GB of VRAM can technically run the smaller Qwen3 models, such as the 8-billion-parameter version, though it might be tight if you want to use the full context window or the "thinking" reasoning mode. If you are referring to the larger Qwen3.8-27B model, the 16 GB card is just barely enough to run a heavily compressed version of it, but it will likely run slowly.

However, this hardware specification alone is not enough to predict whether you will have a good voice-assistant experience. A voice assistant requires extremely fast response times to feel natural, and local inference on a card like the 4060 Ti can be quite slow compared to cloud services. Additionally, a voice assistant needs a separate speech-to-text engine to hear you and a text-to-speech engine to talk back, which consumes your CPU and system RAM rather than just the GPU. Finally, the "goodness" of the experience depends heavily on how well the model understands your specific accent, background noise, and the complexity of the commands you give it.
