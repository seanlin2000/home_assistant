ASSISTANT: When you are running a large language model locally, the most important constraint is almost always how much data fits into your VRAM. If the model is too big for your graphics card, your computer has to offload parts of it to the much slower system RAM, which can cause the text generation to crawl to a near-standstill. So, having a slightly slower GPU with enough memory to hold the entire model in fast VRAM is almost always better than a faster card that forces you to split the model across multiple devices.
