During the decode phase - every token after the first - the GPU spends most of its time waiting on memory reads, not crunching the numbers. A card with 60% more TFLOPS and the same bandwidth generates tokens at very nearly the same speed as the slower card. A card with 78% more bandwidth and similar compute generates them close to 78% faster. The TFLOPS column is mostly a vanity stat for inference workloads - training, of course, is a different matter. The caveat: prefill is compute-bound Prefill is what happens before any tokens come back - the model reads and embeds your prompt to build the KV cache. On a short chat prompt it is invisible. On a 16k-token context with a big RAG document it absolutely is not, and it scales with TFLOPS rather than GB/s. Interactive chat rarely cares; agentic workflows that re-process long contexts every turn very much do.
