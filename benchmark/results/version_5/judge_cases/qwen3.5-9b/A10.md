ASSISTANT: When running large language models locally, the model and its context window must fit entirely into VRAM to avoid slow data transfers between the graphics card and system memory. If a GPU is extremely fast but lacks enough VRAM to hold the model, it will be forced to offload layers to system RAM, which is orders of magnitude slower, making raw processing speed irrelevant. Therefore, ensuring you have sufficient VRAM capacity is usually the most critical factor for actually running the models you want at usable speeds.
