Fix the overthinking default first This is the #1 gotcha, and it will ruin your first impression if you skip it. Qwen3.8-27B ships with thinking on and reasoning_effort defaulting to xhigh. On consumer hardware that default is brutal: Simon Willison's pelican-SVG test took 21 minutes and 22,276 reasoning tokens at xhigh, an M4 Pro user's "svg owl" prompt ran 17m12s, and "draw an svg of a circle" produced an unrequested animated "geometric study". Don't turn thinking off entirely by reflex: on genuinely complex work it earns its keep — Willison's bounding-box vision tool worked with thinking on and broke with it off. Medium plus a budget is the sane default; escalate deliberately.
