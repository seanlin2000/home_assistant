ASSISTANT: When running a large language model locally, VRAM is essentially the "workspace" for the model's brain. If your GPU is incredibly fast but doesn't have enough VRAM to hold the entire model, the system will have to offload parts of the model to your system RAM, which is significantly slower and can cause the generation speed to crawl. Having enough VRAM ensures the entire model stays on the GPU, allowing it to run at its full potential.
