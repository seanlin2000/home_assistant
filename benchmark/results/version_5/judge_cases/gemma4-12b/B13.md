Mount Pilatus Golden Round Trip The flagship Lucerne day trip. The Golden Round Trip is a circuit combining four forms of transport: lake steamer from Lucerne across to Alpnachstad, the world’s steepest cogwheel railway to the 2,132-metre summit, an aerial cable car back down to Fräkmüntegg, and a gondola to Kriens followed by bus back to Lucerne. This classic loop is unique to Pilatus — no other mountain in Switzerland offers this variety of transport options in a single excursion. The cogwheel railway (gradient up to 48 degrees) is genuinely vertiginous and runs only May to November. In winter, the mountain is accessed by the cable car side only.
