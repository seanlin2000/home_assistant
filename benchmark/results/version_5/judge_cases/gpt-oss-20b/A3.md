ASSISTANT: A 4‑gigabyte GPU won’t do it. Even though a Mixture‑of‑Experts model only pulls about four billion of its 26‑billion weights into memory for each token, the GPU still has to hold the whole weight tensor that’s been loaded (the 26 billion parameters), the intermediate activations, the key‑value cache for attention, and a few buffers for computation. Those extra tensors add up to several gigabytes on top of the weight slice. In practice, a 26‑billion‑parameter MoE model needs at least 12 – 14 GB of VRAM when quantized to 4‑bit and a modest context length, and more if you use full precision or longer contexts. So a 4‑GB card would run out of memory almost immediately.
