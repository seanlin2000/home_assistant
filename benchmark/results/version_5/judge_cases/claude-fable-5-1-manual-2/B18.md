The tradeoff is that quality scales with total parameters, memory with parameters times bits per weight, and speed with bytes read per token: a small dense model at high precision is fast and clean, a big dense model squeezed to two or three bits fits but degrades, and a mixture of experts gives big-model knowledge at small-model speed as long as every expert lives somewhere.
