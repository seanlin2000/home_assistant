# Turn 1

USER: My plant is growing new leaves quickly near the top, but its older lower leaves are gradually yellowing and dying. The plant is otherwise healthy. What are the most likely explanations, and what information would you want before deciding whether this is normal or a problem?

ASSISTANT: The most common explanation is normal aging: the plant is putting its energy into new growth and retiring its oldest leaves, and a few yellow bottom leaves on a healthy, actively growing plant is usually fine. The main problem to rule out is nitrogen deficiency, since nitrogen moves from old leaves to new ones, so the bottom yellows first and evenly; the others are overwatering or poor drainage stressing the roots, or the lower leaves being shaded out and dropped. Before deciding, I would want to know what kind of plant it is, how many leaves are going and how fast, how often you water and whether the pot drains, when it was last fed or repotted, and whether the yellowing is uniform or blotchy with brown edges or spots. What is the plant, and how often are you watering it?
