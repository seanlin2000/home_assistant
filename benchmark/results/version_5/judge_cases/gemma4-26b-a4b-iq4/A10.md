USER: Explain why VRAM capacity can matter more than raw GPU speed when choosing hardware for a local LLM. Answer in a way that would sound natural if spoken aloud in under 45 seconds.

ASSISTANT: The main reason is that VRAM determines the maximum size of the model you can actually run. If a model is too large to fit into your video memory, it won't run at a usable speed, or it might not run at all, regardless of how powerful your GPU is. 

Think of it like a desk: the GPU speed is how fast you can work, but the VRAM is the size of the desk. No matter how fast you are, if you can't fit your books and papers on the desk, you can't get the job done. For local LLMs, having a bigger desk is usually much more important than being a faster worker.
