## Consecutive pass or Swiss Travel Pass Flex?

## Flex or regular pass? The honest test
Count the days you will actually board a train, boat or bus. If it is nearly every day of your trip — the classic Zurich, Lucerne, Interlaken, Zermatt loop — buy the regular consecutive pass and save the difference. If your trip looks like “fly in, settle into Wengen for a week, do three big excursion days”, Flex is built for you: activate only the days you move, skip the rest.
## FAQ – Swiss Travel Pass Flex
### How does Flex day activation actually work?
After buying you receive your pass, and you activate each travel day yourself (online, before your first ride of the day). A travel day is a calendar day, not 24 hours — a day activated at 6pm ends at midnight. On days you do not activate, nothing is deducted.
### What does Flex cover on non-activated days?
Nothing — on days without activation the pass is dormant and you would buy normal tickets. Museum entry and mountain discounts also apply only on activated travel days.
