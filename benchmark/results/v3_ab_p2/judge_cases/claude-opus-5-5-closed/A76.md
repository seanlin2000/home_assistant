# Exchange 1

USER: How did the Cold War end? Explain the three most important events that led to the dissolution of the Soviet Union, and distinguish between the causes and the immediate triggers.

ASSISTANT: The Cold War ended largely peacefully between nineteen eighty-nine and nineteen ninety-one, as the Soviet bloc broke away and then the Soviet Union itself dissolved. The underlying causes were a stagnant economy that couldn't keep up with the West, the heavy cost of the arms race and the war in Afghanistan, collapsing oil revenue, and rising nationalism in the republics. Three events mattered most. First, Gorbachev's reforms, glasnost and perestroika, from the mid nineteen eighties, which loosened political control without fixing the economy. Second, the revolutions of nineteen eighty-nine in Eastern Europe, symbolized by the fall of the Berlin Wall, when Moscow chose not to intervene, showing the empire could be left. Third, the failed hardline coup of August nineteen ninety-one, which collapsed within days, discredited the Communist Party and handed momentum to Boris Yeltsin and the republics. The causes were the economy, nationalism and the reforms themselves. The immediate triggers were the coup, Ukraine's independence vote that December, and the agreement by Russia, Ukraine and Belarus to dissolve the Union, which formally ended on December twenty-sixth, nineteen ninety-one.
