Reference sketch (what a correct answer must contain):
The Hue Bridge is the local hub: it talks to the bulbs over Zigbee and keeps scenes and automations. Share control by inviting the other people in the Hue app or letting them use it on the home network, add physical Hue switches for anyone without a phone, and connect a voice assistant (Apple Home through the bridge, Alexa, Google Home, or a local controller such as Home Assistant). If the internet goes down: the bridge, its automations, the Hue app on the local network and physical switches keep working; Apple Home with a home hub keeps working locally; cloud voice assistants such as Alexa and Google stop taking commands, and control from outside the home stops. A local voice setup avoids that dependency.

# Exchange 1

USER: I have Philips Hue lights connected to a Hue Bridge. I want multiple people in my apartment to control the lights, including through voice commands. What is the best architecture for this, and what happens if the internet goes down?

ASSISTANT: The simplest and most reliable setup is to keep the Hue Bridge on your home network and give each person the Hue app, so everyone can control the lights from their own phone on the same wifi. You can invite them to your home through the Hue app so they can also control the lights when away. For voice, link the bridge to Alexa, Google Home, or Apple Home, whichever your household uses. Apple Home and Google Home both allow you to share the home with other people. If you also use Home Assistant, it can talk to the bridge directly and give you dashboards, automations, and local voice control. The Hue lights speak Zigbee to the bridge, which is local. If the internet goes down, local control still works: the Hue app on the same wifi, physical dimmer switches and Hue remotes, schedules and automations stored on the bridge, and Apple Home or Home Assistant control, which work locally. What usually fails is cloud-based voice, such as many Alexa and Google commands, and remote access from outside. A Hue dimmer switch is a good backup for guests.
