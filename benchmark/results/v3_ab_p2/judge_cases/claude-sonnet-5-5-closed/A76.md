# Exchange 1

USER: How did the Cold War end? Explain the three most important events that led to the dissolution of the Soviet Union, and distinguish between the causes and the immediate triggers.

ASSISTANT: The Cold War ended because the Soviet Union lost the ability and the will to keep competing, and then it came apart in 1991. I would point to three events. First, Mikhail Gorbachev's reforms from 1985, glasnost and perestroika, which loosened censorship and the economy but unleashed criticism and nationalism he could not control. Second, 1989, when Eastern Europe's communist governments fell, the Berlin Wall opened, and Gorbachev declined to send tanks, which ended the Soviet bloc. Third, the failed hardliner coup in August 1991, which humiliated the central government and made Boris Yeltsin and the republics the real power. Now the distinction. The deep causes were a stagnant, overstretched economy, the cost of the arms race and the Afghan war, falling oil prices in the 1980s, and long-suppressed national feelings in the republics. The immediate triggers were the coup attempt, the independence declarations that followed, especially Ukraine's referendum in December, and the Belarus agreement between Russia, Ukraine and Belarus that declared the union finished. Gorbachev resigned on December twenty-fifth, 1991.
