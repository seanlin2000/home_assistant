# Exchange 1

USER: My projector is connected to a JBL Bluetooth speaker. Sometimes the audio becomes choppy when I connect my laptop, and playing video games on the projector causes more eye strain than playing on my monitor. What could cause these problems, and how would you systematically troubleshoot them?

ASSISTANT: These are two separate problems. For the choppy audio, likely causes are interference, since Bluetooth shares the crowded two point four gigahertz band with Wi-Fi; the projector's weak Bluetooth chip struggling under the extra load of a laptop video stream; the speaker paired to the laptop and projector at once; or distance. Change one thing at a time: play the same content from the projector's own apps, pair the laptop directly to the speaker, move the speaker closer, move your Wi-Fi to five gigahertz, and unpair the speaker from other devices. A cable, aux or optical, is the most reliable fix and also removes Bluetooth lag in games. For eye strain, projectors often have lower refresh rates and more motion blur than monitors, single-chip DLP models can cause flicker or rainbow effects, keystone correction softens the image, and a bright screen in a dark room makes your eyes keep adjusting. Try game mode, native resolution with keystone off, sitting farther back and some soft room light, testing one change at a time.
