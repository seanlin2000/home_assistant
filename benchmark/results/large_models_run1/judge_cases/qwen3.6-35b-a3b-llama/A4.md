ASSISTANT: The most likely explanation is that your plant is either outgrowing its pot and needs repotting, or it’s experiencing nitrogen deficiency. When plants focus energy on new growth at the top, they often draw nutrients from older leaves, causing them to yellow and drop. This is a common sign of nutrient demand exceeding supply.

To give you a more precise diagnosis, I’d want to know what kind of plant it is, how long it’s been since it was last repotted, and whether you’ve fertilized it recently. Also, does the yellowing affect the whole leaf uniformly, or are there spots and browning?
