## About Bainbridge Island
Reach Bainbridge Island by hopping the Washington State Ferries run from Seattle's Colman Dock (Pier 52) to Winslow, a roughly 35-minute crossing of Puget Sound with the Seattle skyline behind you and the Olympic Mountains ahead. Walk-on passengers never need a reservation, and the fare is charged westbound only so the trip back to Seattle is free. Bainbridge is a green, walkable island whose downtown, Winslow, sits steps from the dock, making it one of the easiest car-free day trips in the Pacific Northwest.
