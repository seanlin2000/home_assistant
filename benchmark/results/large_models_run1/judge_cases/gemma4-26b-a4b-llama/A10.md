USER: Explain why VRAM capacity can matter more than raw GPU speed when choosing hardware for a local LLM. Answer in a way that would sound natural if spoken aloud in under 45 seconds.

ASSISTANT: The main reason is that your model's entire size, including all its parameters, has to fit inside your VRAM to run efficiently. If a model is too large for your available memory, it won't just run slower; it often won't run at all, or it will be forced to offload to your system RAM, which is significantly slower. Think of VRAM like the size of your workbench. You can have the fastest tools in the world, but if your workbench is too small to hold all your materials, you're going to spend more time moving things around than actually working. For LLMs, having enough capacity to hold the model is usually the first hurdle you have to clear before speed even becomes a factor.
