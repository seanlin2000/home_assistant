## The thinking switch
Qwen 3.8 defaults to reasoning effort `xhigh` and will spend tens of thousands of tokens on a trivial question. `--reasoning off` (`LLAMA_ARG_REASONING=off` above) turns thinking off for the whole server. For a single API request, `"reasoning_effort": "none"` in the body does the same, since llama-server turns it into thinking off before the chat template sees it.
