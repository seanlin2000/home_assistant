ASSISTANT: In a 500 square foot room, the microphones matter more than anything else. Look for an array of at least two to four microphones so the device can beamform toward your voice, paired with good echo cancellation so it can hear you while it's talking, which is what makes natural back-and-forth and interrupting possible.
