### Can I visit both Mount Pilatus and Mount Rigi in one day from Lucerne in 2026?
Combining Mount Pilatus and Mount Rigi in a single day from Lucerne remains technically possible in 2026 but creates an exhausting itinerary allowing only 60-90 minutes at each summit, eliminating time for hiking, dining, or absorbing the Alpine experience each mountain deserves.
