## FAQs
### How do high-yield savings accounts work?
High-yield savings accounts offer significantly higher interest rates than traditional savings products, usually 10 times or more. HYSAs are also generally offered by online banks without physical branches, and they may require more steps to withdraw or deposit cash.
### How often do interest rates change?
Interest rates on high-yield savings accounts are variable and can fluctuate daily. In general, though, they change every few months after the Fed meets to adjust the federal funds rate. When the fed funds rate increases, HYSA rates usually follow suit, and vice versa.

# Capital One 360 Performance Savings review
By the HearSmart research desk · Updated November 8, 2026
Capital One 360 Performance Savings suits people who like the rates of an online bank but still want somewhere to walk in. The account paid 3.20% APY on all balances, effective November 6, 2026, with no fees to open or maintain it and no minimum balance. You can open one in about 5 minutes online, in the Capital One Mobile app or at a branch or Café, and you get 60 days after approval to fund it. Automatic savings transfers and mobile check deposit are built in. You cannot withdraw from savings at an ATM; Capital One says to transfer to a checking account first, such as its no-fee 360 Checking. Capital One also notes that Discover Bank deposit accounts opened on or after May 18, 2025 count together with Capital One accounts for FDIC coverage, which matters if you hold money at both. The rate is fair but not top tier.
