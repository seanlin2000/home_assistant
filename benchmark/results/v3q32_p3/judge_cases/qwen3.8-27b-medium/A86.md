Smart homes are meant to make life easier, right up until the internet connection drops and Alexa suddenly stops listening. The drawbacks then become obvious fast: smart lights may disappear from their apps, and security cameras can lose their live feeds.
The good news is that an internet outage does not necessarily bring the whole house to a halt. Many devices retain basic controls or physical fallbacks, while others lose only their remote features.
## What kind of outage is it?
An internet outage is different from losing Wi-Fi altogether. Your router may still be running and keeping devices connected to one another, even if it can no longer reach the wider internet. In that situation, products with local controls or automations may carry on largely as normal.
## What becomes less smart?
Cloud-based features are usually the first to disappear: Voice assistants may stop answering you and remote app access can fail.
Security cameras with local storage may continue saving footage, although you probably will not be able to check it from outside the home.
Automations are less predictable. A schedule stored on a hub may keep running, while a routine handled by Alexa or Google Home could stop completely.
## Why hubs and Matter can help
A dedicated smart home hub can make your setup more resilient by storing controls and automations inside the home.
Systems such as Philips Hue, Homey, and some Tapo or Aqara hubs, can keep devices talking locally, even when the wider internet is unavailable.
Matter can help here too, as compatible devices are designed to communicate over the local network rather than sending every command to the cloud.
## How to prepare your smart home
Future purchases deserve the same test: Look for local control, physical buttons, onboard storage, and clear information about what happens when the cloud is unavailable for whatever reason.
Beyond the occasional wobble, offline support can also signal whether a product remains useful years later, once its manufacturer stops updating the app or closes the service behind it.
