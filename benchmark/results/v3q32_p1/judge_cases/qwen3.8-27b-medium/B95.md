ASSISTANT: Under a thousand dollars, the whole thing runs on one machine, and your real constraint is the local LLM—that's where the money goes. The biggest limitation is that an eight-billion model is a weak reasoner, so expect shallow answers, and you can't run a big model and fast audio at once. Build the bare loop first—wake, listen, answer, speak—then bolt on search and Home Assistant. Prices drift, so treat that hardware line as an estimate.
