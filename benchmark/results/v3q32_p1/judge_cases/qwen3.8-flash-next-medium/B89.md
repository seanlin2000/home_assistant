#### SambaNova funding rounds
Apr 2021 | Series D | SoftBank Group (lead); Temasek; GV; GIC; Walden International; BlackRock; Celesta Capital; Intel Capital | $676M | $5.1B | SambaNova Systems is a full-stack AI infrastructure company founded in 2017 by Stanford professors Kunle Olukotun and Chris Ré, along with former Oracle executive Rodrigo Liang. The company develops proprietary AI chips, hardware systems called DataScale, and integrated software for enterprise AI workloads, competing with Nvidia, Cerebras Systems, and Graphcore. In April 2021, SambaNova closed a $676 million Series D round led by SoftBank Vision Fund, with participation from investors including BlackRock, Celesta Capital, GIC, GV, Intel Capital, Temasek Holdings, and Walden International, achieving a post-money valuation of $5.1 billion. This round brought total funding to over $1 billion at the time, supporting expansion of its AI hardware and a shift toward cloud-based software offerings. Shortly before the round, in December 2020, SambaNova emerged from stealth and launched Dataflow-as-a-Service, an on-demand subscription model allowing enterprises to access its AI applications without managing underlying systems. The funding aimed to scale sales and delivery of these software services alongside hardware. SambaNova generates revenue through DataScale hardware sales, professional services (25-33% of new customer engagements for data prep, model training, and optimization), and subscriptions to pre-trained models via Dataflow-as-a-Service. Later milestones include reaching $100 million annual revenue by June 2025, with total funding exceeding $1.4 billion across multiple rounds, including a Series E in 2026.

## Overview
SambaNova Systems builds an integrated AI computing platform spanning custom reconfigurable dataflow processors, systems, and software. Founded by Stanford researchers Rodrigo Liang, Kunle Olukotun, and Chris Ré, the company sells enterprise and sovereign AI infrastructure intended to reduce the cost and complexity of deploying large models. It completed a $1B first close of its Series F in July 2026 at an $11B post-money valuation.
