## Under an hour: the easy half-days
Mount Pilatus is the classic. From Lucerne, take the boat or train to Alpnachstad for the cogwheel railway — the steepest rack railway in the world at a 48% gradient — or the bus to Kriens for the cable car, which runs all year. The railway only operates from May to November depending on snow, and it closes for scheduled maintenance in spring and autumn, so check before booking anything in April or late October. Return fares by the classic “Golden Round Trip” (boat up, cable car down, or vice versa) run somewhere around CHF 100–115 without a rail pass, less with a Swiss Travel Pass discount.
