ASSISTANT: When running a local Large Language Model, VRAM capacity matters far more than raw processing speed because the model itself is massive; its weights often exceed 20 gigabytes just to sit idle on your drive. Your GPU can only load a slice of the model into memory at once based on its VRAM size, which means you get either a faster inference for a smaller model or a slower but fully capable experience with a larger one. If your VRAM is too small, you cannot even load the most popular models to run locally, regardless of how fast your GPU can crunch numbers.
