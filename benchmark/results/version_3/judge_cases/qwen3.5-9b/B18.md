ASSISTANT: For around 16 GB of GPU memory, the most realistic Qwen models are the 4B and 7B variants when quantized, as they comfortably fit within your budget while maintaining decent quality. The 14B model can be run with significant performance loss using aggressive quantization (like Q3 or Q4), but it’s on the edge of feasibility. Larger models like the 32B require at least 48 GB to run smoothly even with quantization, so they’re not practical for your setup right now.

Here’s the tradeoff: smaller models like Qwen-7B (or newer Qwen3-7B) in Q4 quantization take about 15–17 GB of VRAM and are fast enough for everyday tasks without noticeable lag. Dropping to Q3 quantization saves a bit more memory but reduces reasoning quality slightly—especially on complex math or coding questions. The 4B version is even lighter (around 9–10 GB at Q4) and perfect for quick chats or as a fallback when you’re juggling other apps, though it’s noticeably less capable than the 7B. If you need better reasoning but can’t stretch your memory further, consider using the 32B model in Q4 only when you’ve freed up RAM on system memory—but that’s a stretch compared to the 7B option for most users.
