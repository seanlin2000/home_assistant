ASSISTANT: For local large language models, having enough VRAM is usually the most critical factor because the model itself has to live entirely inside your graphics card's memory. If your GPU runs fast but doesn't have enough video memory to hold the entire model, you'll either be forced to use a slower part of the system memory or split the model across multiple cards, which adds complexity and can still bottleneck performance. Think of it like having a very powerful engine in a car that's too small for your family; no matter how fast it goes, you simply won't have room for everyone.
