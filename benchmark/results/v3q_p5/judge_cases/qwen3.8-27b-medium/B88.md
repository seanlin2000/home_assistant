## 13. Open Source vs Open Weights: Check the License
The phrase “open source” can hide important differences. A model may publish weights but impose additional use conditions. Another may use a recognized permissive license, such as MIT or Apache 2.0, that generally gives users broader freedom, subject to the license text and applicable law. Some releases include a separate acceptable-use policy that also governs deployment.
