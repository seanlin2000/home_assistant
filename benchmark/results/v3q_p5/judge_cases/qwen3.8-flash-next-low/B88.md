## 01 — Three SourcesThree sources, and what each one actually confirms.
A model release in 2026 arrives through at least three documents with different authors and different reliability. The vendor's post is the authoritative statement of what shipped and at what price. The marketplace listing is a serving provider's record of what it can route, with text usually supplied by the vendor and dates set by the listing system. The model card on Hugging Face is the only one that proves weights exist, because it sits next to the files.
For Hy4 the three line up on some points and diverge on one. Tencent's post and OpenRouter's listing agree on the date, November 4, 2026, on vision input and on the list price; the 1M context appears in OpenRouter's listing, not in Tencent's post. They diverge on weights. OpenRouter's model page shows a "Model weights" link to huggingface.co/tencent/Hy4; that address returned HTTP 401 on November 5, and Tencent's post says the weights are planned.
