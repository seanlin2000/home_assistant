The usual rule of thumb is that refinancing only pays off when you can cut your rate by around a full percentage point or more, because closing costs typically run two to five percent of the loan balance and have to be earned back before you come out ahead. If you're already at, say, 6.5 percent, refinancing today would likely cost you money.
