[5] Luka Doncic, Jalen Brunson Named NBA's Players Of The Week
URL: https://www.hoopsrumors.com/2026/11/luka-doncic-jalen-brunson-named-nbas-players-of-the-week.html
Lakers guard Luka Doncic has been named the Western Conference Player of the Week, while Knicks guard Jalen Brunson has won the award in the East, the NBA announced on Monday (via Twitter).
Doncic and Brunson, who were teammates together in Dallas for four years (2018-22) to open their careers before Brunson signed with New York as a free agent in the summer of 2022 (Doncic was traded to the Lakers in February 2025), both led their teams to undefeated weeks. The Lakers were 3-0 when Doncic played (4-0 overall) and the Knicks went 3-0.
Slovenian star Doncic scored at least 40 points in all three of his appearances last week, highlighted by a 53-point outburst against Portland on Friday. He averaged a staggering 45.0 points, 8.3 assists, 7.0 rebounds and 1.3 steals on .525/.395/.829 shooting splits in those three games and is averaging an NBA-best — and career-high — 40.1 PPG through nine appearances, with an elite shooting line of .498/.419/.873.
Brunson, who has been an All-Star and All-NBA member each of the last three seasons, averaged 27.7 PPG, 6.3 APG (only 0.7 TOV) and 3.3 RPG on a strong .483/.429/.750 shooting line last week. The reigning NBA Finals MVP has helped the defending champion Knicks win five of their last six games following an 0-3 start to 2026/27.
It’s the 19th time Doncic has been named Player of the Week and the 10th time Brunson has earned the honor. It’s also the second time they’ve both won the award in the same week — they did so last November too.
Deni Avdija (Trail Blazers), LaMelo Ball (Timberwolves), Stephen Curry (Warriors), Shai Gilgeous-Alexander (Thunder), Victor Wembanyama (Spurs) and Doncic’s teammate Austin Reaves were the other nominees in the West, per the league (Twitter link), while Nickeil Alexander-Walker (Hawks), Giannis Antetokounmpo and Klay Thompson of the Heat, Sixers forward Jaylen Brown and Brunson’s teammate Karl-Anthony Towns were nominated in the East.
