Two things worth flagging. First, "50 percent cumulative dilution" is read here as your percentage ownership being halved, which is the usual meaning; if instead it meant the founders' pool was diluted by 50 percent on a fully-diluted basis in a different way, the number would shift. Second, this ignores any liquidation preference from those later rounds — in practice, preferred investors often take their money off the top before common holders see anything, and with new money coming in at rising valuations, that could meaningfully reduce what's left for your stake. If you hold plain common equity or a safe with no preference stack ahead of you, then 8.3 times is the honest answer.
