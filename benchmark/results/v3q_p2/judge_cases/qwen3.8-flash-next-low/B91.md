ESPN caught a lot of flak for describing Amen Thompson as a 3-and-D player when they made their 2026-27 basketball rankings, and rightfully so. Thompson is a career 21.9% shooter from deep, so the long ball stands out as his biggest weakness. They got something right, though, and that is that D-and-no-3 player is one of the best players in the NBA, which is why the Rockets paid him handsomely right before training camp.
## 16. Cooper Flagg, Dallas Mavericks, PF
Flagg stuffed the stat sheet, evidenced by his averages of 21.0 points, 6.7 rebounds, and 4.5 assists. This came despite the fact that Flagg was playing on a depleted Mavericks team where he was often forced out of position. Flagg defended at a high level right away, he got his teammates involved in his first season, and he proved to be a trustworthy, go-to scorer in his first season. ## How NBA Standings, Play-In and Tiebreakers Work
### NBA tiebreaker procedure
When two teams finish with the same record, the NBA breaks the tie in this order:
- Head-to-head record
- Division record, if both are in the same division
- Conference record
- Record against playoff-eligible teams in their own conference
- Record against playoff-eligible teams in the other conference

## ### How are NBA standings decided?
By winning percentage. Equal records are split by the NBA tiebreaker procedure: head-to-head record first, then division winner, division record, conference record, record against playoff teams, and finally point differential.
