At a one billion dollar exit, that stake is worth about $166,700 before taxes. That's a return of about 8.3 times your money, for a profit of roughly $146,700.

One thing worth flagging: this assumes the dilution simply halves your percentage and that you hold plain common or straight preferred stock with no liquidation preference or participation stacked in front of you.
