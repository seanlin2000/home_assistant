About this crossing
Foot passengers settle the whole round trip at Colman Dock, the terminal on Seattle's central waterfront at Pier 52. WSF sells walk-on tickets as returns and takes no passenger fare at Bainbridge, while drivers buy a one-way vehicle ticket at each end. Cyclists get priority boarding and likewise pay only on the Seattle side. The run takes 35 minutes and leaves from the same dock as the longer route to Bremerton. Walk-on travellers bound for Port Angeles can carry on from the Bainbridge terminal on Clallam Transit's Strait Shot bus through Sequim, but the bus does not wait for a late ferry, so leave a margin. ## About the terminal
Two crossings leave the downtown waterfront, both bound for the Kitsap Peninsula: 35 minutes to Bainbridge Island and an hour to Bremerton. Neither serves a place cut off from the road network, Bainbridge being bridged to the peninsula at Agate Pass, so both boats compete against a long drive south around Puget Sound through Tacoma rather than replacing one altogether. Fares appear on the westbound crossings out of Seattle and not on the runs back, and neither route takes vehicle reservations. The city's other slip, Fauntleroy in West Seattle, lies 10 km south and serves an entirely different set of places.

It changes by weekday vs weekend and by season. Check the live schedule the morning you travel, and be in the terminal earlier than you think — especially after dinner.
