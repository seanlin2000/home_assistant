## Frequently Asked Questions
### What is a Home Assistant voice satellite?
A voice satellite is a small microphone-and-speaker endpoint that puts Home Assistant's Assist voice control in a room while the intelligence runs elsewhere. HA's docs describe satellites as devices that add Assist with wake words to all your rooms: the satellite hears the wake word, streams your command over Wyoming to the HA server, and plays back the reply once the server's speech-to-text, intent handling, and text-to-speech finish. Because it is just an audio endpoint, the hardware can be a $13 ESP32 puck or a retired Raspberry Pi, and one server feeds every room. It is the local-first answer to scattering Echo Dots through a house — with Best Home Assistant Wall Control Panels 2026: SONOFF Wins as the screen-based sibling.
### Can Home Assistant Voice really replace Alexa?
Yes, for technical users with a running HA server. Wake-word detection, common commands, lighting, media playback, and automations all work locally in 2026. What you give up is third-party Alexa Skills and shopping integrations. What you gain is every voice command staying on your LAN — no subscription, no Amazon cloud dependency.
