ASSISTANT: VRAM capacity is critical for local LLMs because the entire language model must fit into the video memory to run efficiently. If the model is too large for your VRAM, it has to constantly offload parts of itself to your slower system RAM, which drastically slows down generation. So, having enough VRAM to hold the whole model is often more important for speed than having a slightly faster core processing unit.
