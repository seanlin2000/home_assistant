The main trade-off revolves around quantization, which is how the model's weights are compressed to fit into your available memory. For example, one source suggests that a four-bit quantized build of the Qwen three-point eight twenty-seven billion model can use about thirteen point two seven gigabytes of memory, leaving room for context. Lower quantization, or using a larger model, generally results in higher quality and more complex reasoning but will consume more VRAM and may slow down inference if it has to spill data into system RAM. Conversely, higher quantization or smaller models will fit easily but sacrifice some of the model's reasoning capabilities and fluency. Speed is also tied to memory, as keeping more of the model's parameters entirely within the GPU's VRAM allows for faster processing.
