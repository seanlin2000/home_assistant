## Winter season starts at Riggli on 31 October
Schilthorn Cableway Ltd announced that the Riggli chairlift will open on Saturday, 31 October 2026, together with the slope it serves. The operator describes the facility as the starting point for winter operations in the Mürren-Schilthorn ski resort.
For the first three weekends, alpine skiing is planned on Saturdays and Sundays. Continuous winter operations are scheduled to begin on 21 November 2026, while the Pända Snowpark is due to remain available to visitors on weekdays during the initial period. The operator announcement also identifies the Engetal slope as the location of the opening.
## Schilthorn links a high summit with winter sport
The combination of a snowfarming park and the Riggli slope brings the season start to a specific part of the mountain rather than presenting the entire ski area as open at once. That distinction matters for travellers planning an early visit, because the operator has announced a staged start: weekend skiing first, followed by continuous winter operations from 21 November 2026.
