## How NBA Standings, Play-In and Tiebreakers Work
### NBA tiebreaker procedure
When two teams finish with the same record, the NBA breaks the tie in this order:
- Head-to-head record
- Division record, if both are in the same division
- Conference record
- Record against playoff-eligible teams in their own conference
- Record against playoff-eligible teams in the other conference

## ### How are NBA standings decided?
By winning percentage. Equal records are split by the NBA tiebreaker procedure: head-to-head record first, then division winner, division record, conference record, record against playoff teams, and finally point differential.
