The connective tissue is Home Assistant's Wyoming protocol, which is just a small standard for streaming audio between satellites and the server so wake-word detection, speech-to-text, and text-to-speech are swappable components rather than baked into one monolith.

**The speech pipeline, stage by stage**

Wake-word detection runs on the satellite itself using openWakeWord or microWakeWord, so nothing is transcribed until you actually address it. That is the single most important privacy decision in the whole system: audio never leaves the room as text unless you said the wake word.
