# Exchange 1

USER: I invest $20,000 in a startup at a $60 million post-money valuation. The company subsequently raises several funding rounds, resulting in 50% cumulative dilution. If the company eventually sells for $1 billion, what is my investment worth before taxes? Calculate my multiple and profit.

ASSISTANT: You own one percent of the company at first — twenty thousand out of sixty million. After fifty percent dilution that stake shrinks to half a percent, which on a billion dollar sale is five million dollars. That's a two hundred and fifty times multiple on your money, for a profit of four point nine eight million before taxes.

One caveat: I assumed the later rounds diluted everyone pro rata, so you kept holding one percent pre-dilution each time. If you didn't participate in any of those rounds, half a percent is right; if some of the dilution hit other investors more heavily, your slice could be a bit bigger.
