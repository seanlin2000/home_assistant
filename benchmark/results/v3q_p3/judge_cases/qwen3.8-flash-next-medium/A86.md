## No internet is not the same as no hub
The two failure modes get merged constantly and they are different questions. A home with internet but no hub keeps local control of its accessories, and on an iPhone running iOS 18 or later can even add Matter accessories; what it never had is automations, notifications, sharing and remote access. A home with a hub but no internet keeps its local control too, and loses the away-from-home half until the connection returns.

Local control does: commands from a phone on the same network travel over your own network, not the cloud, so switching accessories from inside the house can keep working. Remote access, Siri, camera uploads and device sync stop until the connection returns.

Now the outage question. Hue's bulb control is genuinely local: the bridge talks Zigbee to the lamps and LAN to your router, so with the internet down but your router and Wi-Fi still up, the Hue app on a phone connected to that network still controls the lights, and Hue-native scenes, schedules and routines stored on the bridge keep firing on time. What stops is anything that leaves your apartment: controlling the lights from outside, Hue's cloud account features, firmware updates, and notifications. Voice is the fragile layer. Some sources note limited on-device Siri processing on newer iPhones, but the consistent advice is not to depend on any voice assistant as your outage path.
