The architecture is really one "brain" box plus a microphone, with a smart-home server glued on. Your voice flows through five stages, and every stage has a mature open-source piece you bolt together rather than invent. Then text-to-speech, where Piper is the fast, reliable default and Kokoro is the newer, more natural option.
