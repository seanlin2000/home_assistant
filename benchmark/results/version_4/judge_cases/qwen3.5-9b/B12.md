The caveat: prefill is compute-bound Prefill is what happens before any tokens come back - the model reads and embeds your prompt to build the KV cache. On a short chat prompt it is invisible. On a 16k-token context with a big RAG document it absolutely is not, and it scales with TFLOPS rather than GB/s. Interactive chat rarely cares; agentic workflows that re-process long contexts every turn very much do.
