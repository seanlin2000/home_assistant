ASSISTANT: When running large language models locally, your graphics card needs to load the entire model into its memory before it can even start making predictions. If your video memory is too small for the model you want to use, no matter how fast the processor is, the system simply cannot run that specific model at all. You might be forced to use a much smaller, less capable version of the AI just because it fits in memory, whereas a card with more capacity can often load larger models even if they take longer to compute each step. So capacity acts as a hard gatekeeper for what you can run, while raw speed only affects how quickly you get answers once the model is actually loaded into memory.
