ASSISTANT: VRAM capacity often becomes the deciding factor because large language models require the entire model, or at least significant portions of it, to be loaded into the video card's memory to function efficiently. If the model is bigger than the available VRAM, the system has to constantly swap data to the much slower system RAM, which severely bottlenecks performance. Think of VRAM as the model's working desk; if the desk is too small, the model has to keep shuffling papers back and forth, regardless of how fast the processor itself is.
