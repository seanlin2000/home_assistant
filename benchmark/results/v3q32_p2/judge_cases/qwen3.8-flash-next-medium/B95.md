A cheap ESP32 satellite in each room runs the wake word and mic; a single box runs Silero VAD, faster-whisper for speech-to-text, Ollama hosting a quantized 8–14 billion model (Qwen or Llama) as the brain, and Piper or Kokoro for speech out.
