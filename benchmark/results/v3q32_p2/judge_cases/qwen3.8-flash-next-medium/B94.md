What isn't included is the seat. Every passenger on the Glacier Express needs a reservation, and that means you too, pass or no pass. It's CHF 54, and it never moves, whatever class you hold and however far you ride. So a Glacier Express day costs a pass holder CHF 54.
Excellence Class sits outside all of this. It's CHF 540 on top of a first class pass, and it's really a separate product rather than an upgrade you drift into.
