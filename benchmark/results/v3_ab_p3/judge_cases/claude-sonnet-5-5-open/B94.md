## Which one, by trip type
### One base, few long journeys
Staying a week in Interlaken or Zermatt and doing mostly local walks and one or two big rail days. Your ticket spend stays low, and the CHF 150 buys down the few expensive journeys you do make. Also the right answer for a longer stay — a month of half-price travel beats any 15-day pass.
### Moving every day or two
The classic first-timer loop — Zurich, Lucerne, Interlaken, Zermatt, maybe Montreux — crosses the break-even easily, and then the boats, city trams and museums are all free on top. If you are doing the Grand Train Tour in any form, this is not a close call.
## When neither one is worth buying
