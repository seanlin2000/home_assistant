# Exchange 1

USER: My projector is connected to a JBL Bluetooth speaker. Sometimes the audio becomes choppy when I connect my laptop, and playing video games on the projector causes more eye strain than playing on my monitor. What could cause these problems, and how would you systematically troubleshoot them?

ASSISTANT: These look like two separate problems. For the choppy audio, the most likely causes are wireless interference, a weak Bluetooth link, or the laptop doing something different from your other devices. Bluetooth shares the two-point-four gigahertz band with Wi-Fi, so a busy router, a microwave, or distance and obstacles can cause stutter. A laptop may also be using a lower-quality audio mode, or the speaker may be connected to two devices at once. To troubleshoot, change one thing at a time. Move the speaker closer, with a clear line of sight. Switch your Wi-Fi to the five-gigahertz band or use a wired connection. Disconnect other Bluetooth devices. Update the laptop's Bluetooth driver. Test the speaker with a phone, to see whether the laptop is the problem. For the eye strain, projectors are usually less sharp, have lower contrast, and are viewed in a dark room, and the picture is bigger, with more input lag or flicker on some models. Try a dim light behind you, lower the brightness, turn on game mode, check the resolution, and take regular breaks.
