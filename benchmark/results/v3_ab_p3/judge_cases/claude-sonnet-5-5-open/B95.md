ASSISTANT: With a thousand dollars, I would build one small always-on box and keep it simple. The core is a mini PC or a used Mac mini with at least sixteen gigabytes of memory, running Home Assistant for smart-home control, with local speech-to-text using Whisper, local text-to-speech using Piper, and a small language model, around eight billion parameters, served through llama.cpp or Ollama. The biggest limitations are that small models make more mistakes, handle long conversations and tool calls less reliably, and know nothing recent without search, and that voice latency will be slower than commercial assistants. Build in this order. First, voice control of smart-home devices, since that is reliable and useful. Second, natural conversation with the local model. Third, web search.
