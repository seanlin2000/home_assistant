# Exchange 1

USER: How did the Cold War end? Explain the three most important events that led to the dissolution of the Soviet Union, and distinguish between the causes and the immediate triggers.

ASSISTANT: The Cold War ended mostly from the inside, as the Soviet system ran out of money, legitimacy and control. The deep causes built up over decades: a stagnant command economy that couldn't keep pace with the West, the crushing cost of the arms race and the war in Afghanistan, and rising nationalism in the non-Russian republics. Against that background, three events mattered most. First, Mikhail Gorbachev's reforms after nineteen eighty-five, glasnost and perestroika, which loosened censorship and central planning and exposed the system's failures without fixing them. Second, the revolutions of nineteen eighty-nine, when Moscow chose not to send in tanks, the Berlin Wall fell, and the Soviet bloc in Eastern Europe dissolved within months. Third, the failed hardline coup of August nineteen ninety-one, which discredited the Communist Party, elevated Boris Yeltsin, and pushed the republics to declare independence. So the causes were economic decay, military overstretch and nationalism. The immediate triggers were that coup and, in December nineteen ninety-one, the agreement by Russia, Ukraine and Belarus to dissolve the union, followed by Gorbachev's resignation on Christmas Day.
