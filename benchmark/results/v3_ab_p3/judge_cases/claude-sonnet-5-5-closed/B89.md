London-based chip designer Fractile has raised $720 million in a Series C round, betting that memory bandwidth, rather than raw computing power, is the main constraint holding back AI performance.
The funding values the four-year-old company at $7 billion, according to the Financial Times, which cited people familiar with the deal.
Thrive Capital, Founders Fund, Lightspeed, and Redpoint led the round. Other investors include Alphabet’s CapitalG, DST Global, BlackRock, Bain Capital Ventures, T. Rowe Price, Shopify Ventures, Balderton, the UK’s Sovereign AI fund, and the British Business Bank.
The valuation is a sharp jump from May, when Fractile raised around $220 million at a valuation of roughly $1 billion. The new figure puts it among the UK’s most valuable startups, alongside companies such as Revolut.
The Financial Times also reports that Anthropic has expressed interest in becoming an early customer for Fractile’s chips, citing people familiar with the discussions.
Founder Walter Goodwin’s argument starts with a growing imbalance between computing power and memory bandwidth.
Over the past 25 years, chip performance has increased roughly a millionfold, while memory bandwidth has grown only about 40 times. That leaves a 25,000-fold gap between the amount of data processors can calculate and how quickly they can access it.
Goodwin told the FT that today’s frontier models are “drinking through a straw”.
Fractile’s proposed solution combines custom DRAM and logic in a three-dimensional stack. The company says this architecture can deliver substantially higher bandwidth than high-bandwidth memory (HBM), while retaining enough capacity to run large models with long context windows. Goodwin claims it could make inference, the process of generating responses to user queries, up to 10 times faster.
Those performance gains have yet to be demonstrated in a production chip. Fractile has not released a commercial product, and its first chip is planned for next year. For now, the $7 billion valuation is based on its technology and prototypes rather than a product in the market.

### Frequently Asked Questions
What is the Etched Sohu chip, and how does it differ from a GPU?
Sohu is an AI inference accelerator built around two proprietary architectural innovations: Low Voltage Inference, which runs the chip's math blocks at below-half the voltage of conventional AI chips to maximize FLOP density without thermal throttling; and Cluster Scale Memory, which creates a shared low-latency memory pool across chips to eliminate the bandwidth contention between weight reads and key-value cache reads that constrains GPU inference performance. Unlike a GPU, which uses a general-purpose programming model to run any workload, Sohu hard-codes the transformer computation graph into silicon to eliminate the instruction-scheduling and kernel-launch overhead that causes GPU FLOP utilization to fall to 30 to 40 percent on inference workloads.

[4] AI芯片初创Etched官宣：累计融资8亿美元，Transformer专用芯片Sohu即将交付
URL: https://www.seccw.com/Document/detail/id/47002.html
2026年6月30日，AI芯片初创公司Etched对外公布了其融资进展、产品细节及客户合同等关键信息。该公司由三位哈佛大学辍学生（Gavin Uberti、Chris Zhu与Robert Wachen）于2022年在加州创立，累计融资8亿美元，投后估值约50亿美元，并披露已签署超过10亿美元的预售合同。
## Etched公司的**背景与产品定位**
Etched的核心产品是一款名为Sohu的AI推理芯片，采用台积电N4P 4纳米制程工艺制造，搭载144GB HBM3E高带宽内存。与英伟达等厂商的通用GPU不同，Sohu是一款面向Transformer模型专用的ASIC（专用集成电路），其设计原理是将Transformer注意力机制直接固化在芯片电路层面。
据公司披露，Sohu芯片为首次投片即成功（行业术语称为"A0 spin"）。在芯片行业，初创公司通常需要两至三次投片迭代才能获得功能完整的样片。公司目前在加州圣何塞设有原型实验室，在中国台湾设有制造运营团队，员工规模超过400人，技术人员主要来自英伟达、谷歌TPU部门和博通。
以Meta Llama 70B大语言模型为基准，Etched内部实测显示，单台8芯片Sohu服务器的推理吞吐量可达每秒超过50万token。该公司在2024年A轮融资时披露的技术参数称，Sohu的FLOPS利用率可超过90%，而通用GPU运行Transformer推理任务时（基于TRT-LLM框架）利用率约为30%。（以上数据均为公司内部测试或设计指标，未经独立第三方验证。）
Etched的**投资者构成**
Etched的投资方覆盖量化交易和风险投资两大领域。量化交易机构Jane Street、Hudson River Trading、Jump Trading和Two Sigma均参与了融资。风投方面，主要出资方包括Peter Thiel，以及深度学习领域学者Geoffrey Hinton、前特斯拉AI负责人Andrej Karpathy和斯坦福大学教授李飞飞以天使投资人/个人投资人身份参与。此外，与台积电有关联的投资机构VentureTech Alliance亦位列投资者名单之中。
据公司披露，其累计约8亿美元的融资总额中，最近一轮约5亿美元于2025年底至2026年初完成，对应约50亿美元投后估值。
## 技术局限与市场风险
Etched在2026年6月30日的官方新闻稿中声称其系统“正在运行包括DeepSeek、Qwen在内的模型”，但未说明具体版本及支持方式。
然而，在2026年4月，独立技术分析（Spheron）曾明确指出，Sohu的固定功能Transformer架构无法支持采用动态专家路由的MoE模型（如DeepSeek V4、Qwen3-235B-A22B），这是由硬件层面的硬编码限制决定的。
若6月30日的官方声明属实，可能仅支持这些模型的Dense版本，或通过软件模拟/外部处理实现有限兼容，但原生硬件支持MoE架构的可能性较低。Sohu的这一技术路线选择，意味着Etched的商业模式高度绑定于Transformer架构在未来数年内继续占据主导地位的假设。
在商业层面，Etched目前披露的10亿美元预售合同仍处于客户验证阶段。公司表示首批Sohu系统计划于2026年夏季开始交付，但从样品验证到大规模商用部署之间仍存在一定的不确定性。
## 行业定位与竞争格局
Etched的入局是AI推理专用化趋势的延续。在这一赛道上，谷歌已部署自研TPU，亚马逊推出Trainium和Inferentia系列，Groq、Cerebras和SambaNova等初创公司也分别推出了面向特定AI工作负载的专用处理器。Etched的差异化在于其架构选择更为极端：完全放弃可编程性以实现Transformer推理的极致效率。
公司披露的长期规划显示，其目标在2027年前实现吉瓦级（gigawatt-scale）的部署规模。从行业角度看，AI芯片市场正处在通用GPU与专用ASIC两条技术路线的并行发展阶段，Etched的后续量产进展将成为衡量专用路线商业化可行性的重要参照。

[6] Etched raises $500m for a $5bn valuation - report - DCD
URL: https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/etchedai-raises-500m-for-a-5bn-valuation-report/
AI chip startup Etched has reportedly raised $500 million in a funding round that was led by investment firm Stripes and includes participation from existing investor, former PayPal CEO Peter Thiel.
According to a report from Bloomberg, the raise has led to the company being valued at $5 billion. Etched has not yet announced the funding round.
Founded in 2022 by Harvard University dropouts Gavin Uberti and Chris Zhu, the Cupertino-based company has developed an AI chip dubbed Sohu to train, deploy, and optimize transformer models – a type of neural network architecture that underpins large language models (LLMs) such as ChatGPT.
The chips will be produced by TSMC using the manufacturer’s 4nm process, and the company has previously said it’s secured high bandwidth memory and server supply from “top vendors,” although those companies have not been named.
According to Etched, while its algorithm-specific ASIC chips won’t be able to run most traditional AI models, such as deep learning recommendation models (DLRMS) or recurrent neural networks (RNNs), Sohu will be more efficient than traditional GPUs as they execute only the specific transformer models they were designed to perform.
The company has also claimed that Sohu is an “order of magnitude faster and cheaper” than Nvidia’s upcoming Blackwell GPUs, with an eight-chip Sohu server serving more than 500,000 Llama 70B tokens per second. In a 2024 interview with TechCrunch, Etched’s CEO, Uberti, said that one Sohu server would replace 160 H100 GPUs.
