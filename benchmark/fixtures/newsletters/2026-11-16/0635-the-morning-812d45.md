## Opening

By Sam Example

Good morning. Today my colleagues look at an argument over who deserves credit for the widget boom.

## Which Widgets?

Widget makers had a record year: 41 million units sold in the United States, up from 33 million. Now everyone wants the credit.

## The optimists

Optimists, led by the Widget Makers Association, say demand will keep growing as households replace older models.

> "We are only halfway through the replacement cycle," said Dana Ortiz, the association's president.

## The skeptics

Skeptics note that much of this year's demand came from a one-time tax credit that expires in March.

Economists at the University of Example estimate sales could fall by a fifth once the credit ends.
