## Opening

Widget Makers Lead a Rebound

By Pat Market

Good morning. Futures point higher after last week's slide, led by the widget makers.

## Stocks I'm Watching

Widgetron rose 4% in premarket trading after the company raised its full-year revenue forecast to $12.5 billion, from $11.8 billion.

Gizmo Corp. fell 3% after its chief financial officer resigned. The company said the departure was unrelated to its accounting.

Sprocket Holdings was little changed ahead of its earnings report on Tuesday.

## One Big Chart
